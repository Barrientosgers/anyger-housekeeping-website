About Us

Cleaning Homes Like They're Our Own

AnyGer's Housekeeping is a family-owned cleaning business based in Redmond, Washington.

For us, cleaning isn't just about making a room look good. It's about helping people feel comfortable in their homes.

Aida is at the heart of what we do. With more than 10 years of cleaning experience, she's known by clients for thorough, dependable, careful work, and she takes real pride in leaving a home clean. Many of our customers like knowing exactly who is coming into their home and what kind of care to expect.

We're a small, Washington State licensed business built on trust, consistency, and hard work, and we'd be glad to help with yours.
